The route

Five stops from click to cash.

No guesswork — this is exactly how cashback moves.

Read the full guide

  1. Pick an offer

    Choose a partner store on PerkStation and tap Activate cashback.

  2. Shop as usual

    You land on the store's site. Complete your purchase in the same visit.

  3. We track it

    The store reports your order to us and it shows as Tracked, then Pending.

  4. Store approves

    After the return window closes, the store confirms the order and it's Approved.

  5. Withdraw

    Once your approved balance reaches the minimum, request a payout.

Cashback status

Always know where your cashback is.

Every purchase carries one of five statuses. Here's what each means.

Tracked

Tracked

We've recorded your visit and the store has reported your order to us.

Pending

Pending

The store is waiting for its return or cancellation window to close.

Approved

Approved

The store has confirmed the order. The cashback joins your withdrawable balance.

Paid

Paid

You requested a withdrawal and the payout has been sent to you.

Declined

Declined

The store rejected the order — for example, it was returned, cancelled or broke the offer terms.

More answers
What is PerkStation?

PerkStation is a cashback and rewards platform. When you visit a partner store through a PerkStation offer and buy something, the store pays us a commission. We share part of it with you as cashback.

When does cashback become withdrawable?

Only after the store confirms your order — usually once its return or cancellation period has ended. Confirmed cashback changes to Approved and becomes part of your withdrawable balance.

Why could my cashback be declined?

The most common reasons are returned or cancelled orders, coupon codes not listed on the offer, or a purchase that didn't follow the offer terms. See the Cashback Policy for the full list.

Something didn't track. What now?

Keep your order confirmation and contact us through our Contact page or by email at with the store name, order ID and date. The missing cashback guide explains what to include.
