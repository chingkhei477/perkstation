How it works

Your cashback, stop by stop.

Here's how cashback is tracked, what makes a purchase eligible, how approval works, and when you can withdraw.

  1. Pick an offer

    Choose a partner store on PerkStation and tap Activate cashback.

  2. Shop as usual

    You land on the store's site. Complete your purchase in the same visit.

  3. We track it

    The store reports your order to us and it shows as Tracked, then Pending.

  4. Store approves

    After the return window closes, the store confirms the order and it's Approved.

  5. Withdraw

    Once your approved balance reaches the minimum, request a payout.

01 · Tracking

How we know you shopped.

When you tap Activate cashback, we send you to the store through a tracked link. The store, or its affiliate network, records that your visit came from PerkStation.

When you buy something, the store reports the order to us — usually within a few hours, sometimes up to a few days. It then shows in your activity as Tracked and then Pending. The amount may first appear as an estimate and is finalised when the store confirms.

DO

Activate right before you shop

Open the store from PerkStation each time, then check out in that visit.

DO

Keep your order confirmation

You'll need the order ID and date if anything goes missing.

AVOID

Ad-blockers & private windows

They can block the cookies the store uses to credit us.

AVOID

Switching sites mid-way

Visiting another cashback, coupon or price site before checkout can overwrite tracking.

02 · Eligibility

What qualifies for cashback.

Each offer may add its own conditions. These general rules apply to every offer.

Eligible when

  • You started from a PerkStation offer and completed the purchase in the same visit.
  • Cookies were allowed and no ad-blocker or private browsing blocked tracking.
  • You used only coupons listed on the offer (or none).
  • The products and payment method are eligible under the offer terms.
  • The order was not returned, exchanged or cancelled, fully or partly.

Not eligible

  • Orders placed without activating the offer, or in a different visit.
  • Gift cards, wallet top-ups, taxes, shipping and other excluded items (unless the offer says otherwise).
  • Returned, cancelled or exchanged items.
  • Orders using coupons, cashback or rewards from another source not allowed by the offer.
  • Fraudulent, duplicate, bulk or reseller orders.
03 · Approval

Who approves it and when.

The store approves cashback, not PerkStation. It confirms that the order is final — not returned, not cancelled and within the offer terms — and passes that to us. We then mark it Approved.

Tracked

Pending

Pending

The store is waiting for its return or cancellation window to close.

Approved

Approved

The store has confirmed the order. The cashback joins your withdrawable balance.

Paid

Paid

You requested a withdrawal and the payout has been sent to you.

Declined

Declined

The store rejected the order — for example, it was returned, cancelled or broke the offer terms.

Why it takes time: stores usually wait for their return or cancellation window to close before confirming. That's why cashback can stay Pending for a while. Where a store shares an expected approval time, we show it in the offer terms.

Missing cashback

Didn't track? Here's what to do.

Wait at least 72 hours after your purchase. Stores can take that long to report orders. If it still isn't showing, send us a claim.

Raise a missing cashback claim

Include in your claim

  • Store name and order date
  • Order ID and the order amount
  • A screenshot or copy of the order confirmation
  • The email address you use with PerkStation

We pass claims to the store or its affiliate network. The final decision rests with the store.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does PerkStation cost anything to use?

Using PerkStation offers does not add anything to the price you pay at the store. Cashback is funded from the commission the store pays us.

How long does approval take?

It depends on the store. Most stores confirm orders only after their return or cancellation period ends, and then pass the confirmation to us. Where a store shares an expected timeline, we show it in the offer terms.

Can a Pending cashback still be declined?

Yes. Pending means the store hasn't confirmed the order yet. If the order is returned, cancelled or doesn't meet the offer terms, the store may decline it.

Do I earn cashback on the full order value?

Usually cashback is calculated on the amount the store counts as eligible — typically after discounts and excluding taxes, shipping, gift cards and wallet top-ups. The offer terms say what qualifies.

Can I use my own coupon code?

Only coupons shown on the PerkStation offer are safe to use. Other codes can override tracking, and the store may decline the cashback.

What if the store changes the cashback rate?

The rate that applies is the one shown on the offer when you activated it, as long as the store honours it. If a store revises a rate retrospectively, we follow what the store confirms and explain any change to you.
