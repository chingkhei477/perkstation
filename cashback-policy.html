Overview

This Cashback Policy explains how cashback on PerkStation is earned, tracked, approved, declined and withdrawn. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions. Individual offers may add conditions, and the offer's own terms apply where they are more specific.

How cashback is earned

  1. Activate an offer on PerkStation, which opens the store through a tracked link.
  2. Complete your purchase on the store's site in the same visit.
  3. The store reports the order and later confirms whether it is final.
  4. Confirmed cashback becomes Approved and can be withdrawn once you reach the minimum.

Tracking

Tracking relies on cookies and the store's or affiliate network's systems. Orders usually appear within 72 hours but may take longer. To help tracking work:

  • allow cookies and turn off ad-blockers for the visit;
  • avoid private or incognito windows;
  • don't visit other cashback, coupon or price-comparison sites before checkout;
  • use only coupons listed on the offer.

Eligible and excluded purchases

Cashback is normally calculated on the eligible order value — typically after discounts and excluding taxes, shipping, service fees and gift-wrap. Unless an offer says otherwise, these are excluded:

  • gift cards, vouchers, wallet top-ups and recharges where excluded by the store;
  • returned, exchanged or cancelled orders (fully or partly);
  • orders using coupons or rewards from another source not allowed by the offer;
  • orders placed without activating the offer, or in a later visit;
  • bulk, reseller, business or fraudulent orders.

Approval timeline

The store decides whether an order qualifies. Most stores confirm orders only after their return or cancellation period ends and their own checks are complete. Approval time differs by store. Where a store shares an expected timeline, we show it in the offer terms. PerkStation cannot approve cashback before the store confirms it.

Adjustments and declines

The cashback amount may change if the store adjusts the order — for example, after a partial return or a price change. If an order is returned or cancelled, or breaks the offer terms, the store may decline it. If cashback has already been paid and the store later reverses it, we may deduct that amount from your future cashback.

Missing cashback claims

If a purchase hasn't appeared 72 hours after you placed it, contact us through our Contact page or by email at . Include the store name, order ID, order date, order amount and a copy of the order confirmation. Raise claims as soon as possible — many stores accept claims only within a limited period. We pass claims to the store or network, whose decision is final.

If we ever introduce an expiry rule for unused balances, we will announce it on this page and give advance notice before it applies to you.

Misuse and fraud

We may hold, adjust or cancel cashback, and suspend accounts, where we reasonably suspect fraud, manipulation of tracking, multiple accounts or other abuse described in the Terms.

Changes to this policy

Rates and rules can change. The version in force when the store confirms your purchase applies. The "Last updated" date shows the current version.
