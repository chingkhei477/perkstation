How store refunds and returns affect your cashback.

What this policy covers

PerkStation does not sell products or services. We are a cashback platform that refers you to partner stores. This Refund Policy explains what happens to cashback when an order is refunded, returned or cancelled, and how payout errors are handled.

Refunds for purchases

Your purchase is made with the partner store, so refunds, returns, replacements and cancellations are handled entirely by that store under its own policies. Please contact the store directly about any product, payment or delivery issue. PerkStation cannot issue refunds for store purchases.

How refunds affect cashback

  • Full return or cancellation — the cashback for that order is declined.
  • Partial return — the cashback is reduced to match the value you kept, as confirmed by the store.
  • Exchange — stores often treat exchanges as a return, so the original cashback may be declined.
  • Refund after payout — if a store reverses an order after its cashback was paid to you, we may deduct that amount from your future cashback.

Payout errors

If a withdrawal fails or is returned because of a technical problem on our side, we will retry the payout or return the amount to your balance. If you think a payout amount is wrong, contact us through our Contact page or by email at with the details, and we will investigate and correct any error we find. Payouts sent to incorrect details you provided cannot be recovered by us.

Fees

PerkStation does not charge you to activate offers. If we ever introduce a paid feature, its price and refund terms will be shown clearly before you pay.
