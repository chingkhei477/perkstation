About PerkStation

Cashback, with the route on the ticket.

PerkStation exists to make cashback easy to understand — what you can earn, when it's confirmed and how you get paid.

What we do

PerkStation partners with online stores and affiliate networks. When you shop through a PerkStation offer, the store pays us a commission for the referral. We share part of it with you as cashback.

You don't pay anything extra for this. The price at the store is the same; the cashback comes from the commission. Cashback becomes yours once the store confirms the purchase.

The name

A station is where journeys are clear: you see the route, the stops and where you'll arrive.

That's how we think cashback should work — every step visible, from the moment you click to the moment you're paid.

Our principles

What you can hold us to.

Transparency

Offer rates, exclusions and statuses are shown plainly. If a store declines cashback, we tell you why when the store gives a reason.

Genuine offers only

We list only offers we've confirmed with partner stores or networks. No made-up deals, no fake urgency.

Privacy by default

We collect only the data needed to track and pay cashback, and we never sell your personal data.

Fair to everyone

Clear rules protect shoppers and stores. Misuse and fraud are not allowed, so cashback stays possible for everyone.

Helpful support

A missing or declined cashback is followed up with the store. You'll always get a straight answer.

Always improving

Our policies are written to be read. When they change, we update the date at the top of each page.

Get in touch

Questions, partnerships or feedback?

Shoppers, stores and affiliate partners can all reach us through the contact page.
