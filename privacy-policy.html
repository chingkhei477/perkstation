Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PerkStation ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our website and cashback services. By using PerkStation, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account and contact details — such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Payout details — the details needed to send your withdrawals.
  • Support messages — what you send us, including order IDs and screenshots for missing cashback claims.

Information collected automatically

  • Click and tracking data — when you activate an offer, we record the time, the store and a unique click reference so the store can credit your purchase.
  • Transaction data from stores — order reference, order value, commission and status, as reported by stores or affiliate networks. We do not receive your card or bank details from stores.
  • Device and usage data — browser type, device, IP address and pages visited, used for security and to improve the site.

How we use information

  • To track purchases and credit, approve and pay cashback.
  • To respond to your requests and handle missing cashback claims.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and duplicate claims, and to keep the service secure.
  • To send service messages about your cashback or account.
  • To send offers or updates, only where you have agreed. You can opt out at any time.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Cookies and tracking

Cashback depends on cookies. When you activate an offer, the store or its affiliate network sets a cookie so the purchase can be linked back to PerkStation. We also use essential cookies to run the site.

You can control cookies in your browser settings. If you block them, cashback may not track.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed:

  • Partner stores and affiliate networks — click references and order details needed to track and confirm purchases or investigate missing cashback.
  • Payment providers — the details needed to send your withdrawal.
  • Service providers — hosting, email and security providers acting on our instructions.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, or to protect our users, our service or others from fraud or harm.

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above. This includes keeping transaction records for as long as tax, accounting or legal rules require. When we no longer need it, we delete or anonymise it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Keep your account details private.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent to marketing, or close your account. Some information may be kept where the law requires it. To make a request, contact us through our Contact page or by email at .

Children

PerkStation is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party sites

Offers take you to partner stores' websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information. Please review them before you share details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be highlighted on the site.
